## Konzept (EN, Rohtext)
A trinity of black, silver, and flesh converges in sculptural ceremony. Molten metal ear cuffs and ethereal grey fur frame braided geometry and severe tailoring, while mirrored gestures on checkered pedestals propose devotion as kinetic architecture. Here, divinity is not transcendent but tactile — engineered through texture, repetition, and the disciplined body.
